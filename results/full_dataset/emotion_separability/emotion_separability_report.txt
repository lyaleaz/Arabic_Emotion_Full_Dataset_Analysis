METHODOLOGICAL NOTES
- Intra-class and inter-class pairwise cosine means are exact over the full dataset and are calculated from class vector sums.
- Silhouette uses cosine distance on the same fixed stratified sample for both models; it is not computed on all 41,202 rows.
- Centroid cosine similarities compare normalized emotion centroid directions.
- These are label-conditioned geometric diagnostics, not classification accuracy or proof of causal model superiority.
- The overall intra/inter means are pair-weighted; per-emotion values are also provided to expose class imbalance.
