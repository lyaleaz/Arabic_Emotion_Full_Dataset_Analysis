PCA FULL DATASET — METHOD
Every record was used in PCA fitting AND projection; no sampling.
PCA is fit separately per model on mean-centered 768-D embeddings.
Centered 768x768 scatter matrix built in float64 batches; eigendecomposition yields exact principal components up to floating-point error.
Input embeddings and metadata are read only.
Scatter plots contain all 41,202 points per model; overlapping points and transparency can visually hide observations.
PCA axes are model-specific; positions and directions are not directly aligned between the two subplots.
PCA is unsupervised and does not establish classification accuracy.
