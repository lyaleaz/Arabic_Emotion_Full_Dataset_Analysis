Methodological notes:
- Mean pairwise cosine is computed exactly over all unordered pairs using the unit-vector identity; no N x N matrix is created.
- Effective rank and component variance are computed from eigenvalues of the centered feature covariance/scatter matrix.
- The covariance/scatter matrix is accumulated in batches.
- These results describe embedding geometry; they do not alone establish emotion-class separability or model superiority.
